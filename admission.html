Admission Requirements

Parents and guardians should prepare the relevant documents to help the school process an application. The administration will explain the specific requirements for each class.

    A completed school admission application form.

    The learner's birth certificate or other accepted proof of age.

    Previous school reports or academic records, where applicable.

    A transfer or leaving document for learners joining from another school, where applicable.

    Passport-size photographs, if required.

    Parent or guardian contact information.

    Any additional documents requested by the school.

Please confirm the required documents with the school office before submitting your application.

Our Admission Process

We aim to make the admission process clear and convenient for every parent or guardian. Follow these steps to apply for a place at our school.

Step 1: Make an Inquiry

Contact the school office to learn about available classes, school fees, entry requirements, and vacancies.

Step 2: Obtain an Application Form

Collect the admission form from the school office or download it from the school's website if an online form is available.

Step 3: Complete the Form

Fill in the learner's personal details, previous academic information, and the contact details of the parent or guardian.

Step 4: Submit the Documents

Attach the required supporting documents and submit the completed application to the school administration.

Step 5: Admission Review

The school will review the application and communicate whether an interview, assessment, or additional information is required.

Step 6: Confirm Admission

Successful applicants will receive guidance about admission confirmation, school fees, reporting dates, uniforms, and other requirements before classes begin.

Frequently Asked Questions

When can I apply for admission?

Contact the school office to confirm the current application period and whether places are available in your preferred class.

Can a learner transfer from another school?

Transfer applications may be considered depending on the learner's academic records, the class requested, and available space.

How can I find out about school fees?

Request the official fee structure from the school administration. Confirm any additional charges before making payments.

How do I know if my child has been admitted?

The school administration will communicate the outcome of the application and explain the next steps for successful applicants.
